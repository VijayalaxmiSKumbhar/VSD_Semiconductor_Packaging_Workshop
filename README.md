  #### Why is Semiconductor Packaging is needed?

` Packaging can be viewed as the process of giving functionality, protection, and connectivity to a fabricated semiconductor die, enabling its successful operation in real-world electronic systems.`

* Example of semiconductor packaging is `Ball Grid Array (BGA)`

#### The package consists of key elements such as:
  
  `die`, `die attach material`, `wire bonds`, `molding compound`, `substrate`, and `solder balls`.
  
* The `die` provides the core functionality of the integrated circuit. 

* The `die attach material` and `molding compound` provide mechanical support and protection from environmental and physical damage. 

* `Wire bonds` and the `substrate` establish electrical interconnections for signal and power routing. 

* `Solder balls` create the interface between the package and the printed circuit board (PCB), enabling communication with the external system.

## Semiconductor Industry Ecosystem and Manufacturing Flow

## Semiconductor Industry Ecosystem

The semiconductor industry consists of multiple business models that contribute to different stages of the chip development lifecycle.

- **Integrated Device Manufacturers (IDMs)**, such as Intel, Samsung, Micron, Renesas, SK Hynix, Texas Instruments, and STMicroelectronics, perform semiconductor design, wafer fabrication, packaging, testing, and product manufacturing within a single organization.

- **Fabless companies**, including Qualcomm, NVIDIA, AMD, Apple, and MediaTek, focus on semiconductor design and product innovation while outsourcing manufacturing activities.

- **Foundries**, such as TSMC and GlobalFoundries, specialize in wafer fabrication and semiconductor manufacturing for fabless companies and other customers.

- **OSAT (Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test) companies**, including ASE, Amkor, JCET, PTI, and UTAC, provide semiconductor packaging and testing services. Emerging OSAT players in India include Micron, CG Power–Renesas, Tata Electronics, and Kaynes Semiconductor.

### Semiconductor Manufacturing Flow

The semiconductor product lifecycle typically follows these stages:

1. **Design** – Circuit and system architecture development.
2. **Wafer Process** – Fabrication of semiconductor devices on silicon wafers.
3. **Package & Test** – Packaging the fabricated die and performing electrical and reliability testing.
4. **Assembly** – Integration of packaged devices into electronic products and systems.

### OSAT Process Flow

The OSAT stage generally includes:

- **Wafer Test** – Verification of individual dies on the fabricated wafer.
- **Package** – Encapsulation and interconnection of the die within a semiconductor package.
- **Package Test** – Functional, electrical, and reliability testing of the packaged device before shipment.
